EPPT Child Safety Standards

These Child Safety Standards apply to the EPPT app and are published by Eli Felipe ("Service Provider"). EPPT has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Prohibited Content and Behavior

Users must not use EPPT to create, upload, store, share, distribute, promote, or facilitate content or behavior that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • child sexual abuse material (CSAM);
  • grooming or attempting to establish contact with a child for sexual exploitation;
  • sexualization of minors in text, images, video, audio, or digitally generated content;
  • sextortion, blackmail, coercion, or threats involving a minor;
  • trafficking, solicitation, or commercial sexual exploitation of a child; and
  • any other content or conduct that exploits or endangers children.
Enforcement

If the Service Provider becomes aware of content or conduct that may violate these standards, the Service Provider may review the matter, remove or disable access to the content, restrict access to features, suspend or terminate accounts when applicable, and preserve or disclose relevant information to law enforcement or child protection authorities where required by applicable law.

Reporting Concerns

If you believe content or behavior on EPPT may involve CSAE or CSAM, contact the Service Provider immediately at elifelipe@hotmail.com. If a child may be in immediate danger, contact local law enforcement or the appropriate child protection authority without delay.

Child Safety Point of Contact

The designated child safety point of contact for EPPT is Eli Felipe, available at elifelipe@hotmail.com.

Updates

These standards may be updated from time to time. Continued use of EPPT after updates become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised standards.

Effective date: 2026-03-21
